Supporting service members and veterans

We’re committed to connecting our nation’s military to the training, education and resources that put them on the path to financial stability. We employ thousands of veterans and military spouses. Building on that, since 2014 we have hired more than 10,000 service members. We also finance and partner with organizations that foster successful civilian transition.

Bank of America as an employer

Support through mentorship

Bank of America honors its ongoing commitment to veterans and military spouses through a $350,000 grant to American Corporate Partners (ACP). This leading nonprofit supports transitions to meaningful civilian career. This grant supports 250 mentorships, providing guidance on career planning, networking and professional growth.
